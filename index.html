JUST WOMEN Can CHANGE Society

Amplifying Women's Voices for a Brighter Future

Just Women is a women-led media and storytelling initiative creating opportunities for Afghan women and girls through journalism, education, storytelling and digital media.

Explore Stories Join Our Team

OUR PURPOSE

Stories matter when women tell them.

Just Women brings together journalism, education, storytelling and digital media to create space for Afghan women's voices. We believe women should shape the stories told about their lives and communities.
