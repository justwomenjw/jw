Building Skills, Opening Doors

WHY THESE SKILLS MATTER

Areas of learning

Journalism & Alternative Education

With universities closed, we provide practical media and reporting skills so Afghan women and girls can share their stories and build careers in journalism.

GED & Academic Foundation

Many girls are forced to leave school after the 6th grade. Our GED preparation helps them continue their education, build academic confidence, and open new pathways.

Human & Women's Rights

Understanding fundamental human rights is crucial. This knowledge empowers women and girls to know their rights, advocate for equality, and protect their dignity.

Health & Mental Wellbeing

In these challenging times, mental health is a priority. We provide essential knowledge on self-care, emotional resilience, and where to seek support.

Documentary & Visual Storytelling

Learning to record and preserve stories responsibly. We train women in documentary production so they can eventually create and direct their own films.

Community, Hope & Motivation

Through gatherings and conferences with activists, we create spaces for hope, motivation, and dialogue to inspire the next generation of Afghan women leaders.
