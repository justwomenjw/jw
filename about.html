WHO WE ARE

Our Story

Just Women Can Change Society is a media and storytelling initiative founded by Afghan human rights activist and journalist Marwa Habibi. The organization was established to create space for Afghan women's voices in media, education, and storytelling. Our work is guided by principles of editorial integrity, dignity, inclusion, respect, safety, transparency, and accountability. We aim to amplify women's voices and provide a platform for their stories to be heard and shared globally.

WHAT GUIDES US

Mission, Vision & Values

Mission

To amplify women's voices and expand opportunities for Afghan women and girls through media, education, and storytelling.

Vision

A future where women can tell their own stories, access learning opportunities, and participate meaningfully in society.

Values

Editorial integrity, dignity, inclusion, respect, safety, transparency, and accountability.

FOUNDER & LEAD

Marwa Habibi

مروه حبیبی

Human Rights Activist & Journalist

Full professional profile ↗

Marwa Habibi is an Afghan human rights activist and journalist whose work spans independent reporting, documentary translation, poetry, and public speaking. Her writing and interviews have appeared through the BBC World Service, Afghanistan International, Amu TV, and independent literary platforms. She founded Just Women — Can Change Society to create space for Afghan women's voices in media, education, and storytelling.
