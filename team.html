VOLUNTEER WITH US

Skills with Purpose

Just Women welcomes volunteers from Afghanistan and internationally who want to support women-led media, storytelling, and future educational activities. Every contribution is valued, and we aim to match skills with needs carefully.

WHO WE'RE LOOKING FOR

Volunteer roles

Journalists & Reporters

News reporters, investigative journalists, and event reporters covering stories of women and girls.

International Correspondents

Reporting on global events, international news, and issues from around the world.

Editors & Researchers

Fact-checkers, researchers, editors, and proofreaders ensuring accuracy and quality.

Visual Journalists

Photographers, video journalists, and documentary storytellers capturing powerful moments.

GED Educators

Volunteer instructors for Language Arts, Math, Science, and Social Studies.

Web & UI/UX Developers

Full-stack developers and UI/UX designers building our digital platform.

Cybersecurity & Tech Support

Cybersecurity specialists, security testers, and website maintenance volunteers.

Communications & Design

Social media managers, media communications volunteers, and graphic designers.

VOLUNTEER CONDITIONS

What to know

Voluntary and unpaid

Positions are voluntary and unpaid. Contributions are offered freely.

Dependent on needs

Collaboration depends on organizational needs and availability.

No guarantee

Submitting an application does not guarantee selection.
